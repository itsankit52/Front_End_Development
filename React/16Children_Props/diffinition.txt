What is children prop in React?

    children is a special prop in React that lets you pass components or elements between the opening and closing tag of another component.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
What happens?

    <h2> and <p> are passed to Card

    Inside Card, they are available as props.children

They get rendered inside the <div className="card">
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Why children is useful?

    To create reusable wrapper components (Card, Modal, Layout, Button, etc.)

    To make components more flexible

    To avoid repeating layout code
